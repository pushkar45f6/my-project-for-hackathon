Frequently Asked Questions

The Sustainability Score reflects how efficiently your renewable resources are being utilized, integrating generation, efficiency, and performance trends into a single indicator.

Generation estimates are based on weather conditions, historical production, active renewable capacity, and performance trends across the monitored resource portfolio.

Low-generation alerts usually indicate a period of reduced solar or wind availability, weather constraints, maintenance issues, or sudden changes in expected operating efficiency.

GreenLink combines historical usage patterns, local weather data, and current resource performance to estimate near-term generation and consumption changes.

Use the Ideas & Innovation section to share a concept, improvement opportunity, or conservation suggestion. Teams can review it for viability and impact.

Use the Contact Support or Report a Problem actions to document the incident and notify the platform administrators for review.
